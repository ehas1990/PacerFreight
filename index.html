Groupage (LCL)

Our international ocean groupage services bundle your smaller shipments into shared sea containers, unlocking premium global routes at a fraction of the cost. You only pay for the exact space you use. With fixed weekly sailing schedules, secure consolidation, and seamless port-to-port tracking, we make worldwide ocean freight fast, affordable, and accessible for businesses of any size.
